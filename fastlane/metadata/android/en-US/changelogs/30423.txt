Ares release candidate 6.

- NextGen sends attachments like Kyllene: images go to the model as images; files over 12 MB stream to the gateway (up to 100 MB each).
- Save and Share now download delivered files up to 1 GB, with progress.
- Model settings (main, fallback, auxiliary) are in NextGen Settings and the model picker.
- Classic-only settings are labelled. Launcher label is now "Ares".

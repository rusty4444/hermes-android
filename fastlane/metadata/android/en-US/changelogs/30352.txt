In-app APK download and installation for official GitHub releases.

- Tap Update now on supported Android installs to download the matching ABI
  APK with live progress and cancellation.
- Verifies HTTPS download host, byte count, SHA-256 checksum, package name,
  production signing certificate, ABI compatibility and strictly newer
  versionCode before launching the system installer.
- Preserves a verified download across the unknown-sources permission trip.

Chat pins now sync with Hermes Desktop.

- Pin or unpin a chat here and it shows on Desktop, and the other way round.
- Pinned chats lead Chats in a shared Pinned section.
- Archiving a pinned chat also unpins it, as on Desktop.
- Pins this phone saved on its own in earlier versions move to the server
  automatically.
- On Hermes servers too old for synced pins, Pin stays hidden until Hermes is
  updated.
